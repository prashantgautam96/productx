### 2. **AI-Powered Skill Suggestions**

When AI is enabled, the system:
1. Analyzes the job description
2. Reviews your experience and projects
3. Suggests 5-8 additional skills that:
   - Match job requirements
   - Are current market standards
   - Can be inferred from your experience
   - Improve ATS matching

**Example Suggestions:**
- If you have "REST API" experience → suggests "ML Model APIs"
- If you have "Distributed Systems" → suggests "Distributed ML Systems"
- If you have "Python" → suggests "Python (ML)", "Data Science"
- Always suggests: "Machine Learning", "MLOps", "Model Deployment"
